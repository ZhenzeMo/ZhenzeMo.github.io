Zhenze Mo

Hi, my name is Zhenze Mo (莫镇泽). I study the relationship between human cognition and machine intelligence, using computational methods to understand human behavior and to design more interpretable and adaptive AI systems. I am a Master’s student at the Khoury College of Computer Sciences, Northeastern University.
